# Model Card — GeoGuess EfficientNet-B0

This card documents the committed checkpoint `data/best_model_efficientnet_b0.pt`, which is the model used for all statistical analysis in the notebook and in this repository's README. A separate, older checkpoint (`data/best_model.pt`) is also committed for transparency — see [Deployed vs. committed checkpoint](#deployed-vs-committed-checkpoint) below and `DEPLOYMENT.md` for why.

## Model Details

- **Architecture.** EfficientNet-B0 (~5.3M parameters), loaded from `timm` with ImageNet pretrained weights. Backbone is used as-is; a fresh 180-class linear head is added on top of the pooled feature vector.
- **Task.** Single-label country classification from a 224×224 street-view image.
- **Classes.** 180 countries (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes). All 180 had ≥20 training images in the pulled sample, so the `min_images_per_class: 20` filter dropped none of them — the committed head covers all 180.
- **Input.** JPEG images resized to 224×224, ImageNet normalization. No TTA at evaluation time.
- **Output.** Softmax over 180 classes; top-1 prediction is the committed label.

## Training Data

- **Source.** [OpenStreetView-5M](https://huggingface.co/datasets/osv5m/osv5m) (Astruc et al., 2024). Geo-tagged Mapillary imagery filtered for country balance.
- **Sampling.** 10 shards of the official `train` split, stratified-sampled for a combined train+val pool. 15,000 images carved out for validation (seed 42); the remaining **484,799 images** are the training set. 5,000 images sampled from the official `test` split for held-out evaluation.
- **Class filter.** The `min_images_per_class: 20` filter is applied before sampling. For this run it dropped zero classes — all 180 countries had enough images in the pulled shards to clear the threshold.
- **Benchmark holdout.** An additional 1,000 images from the official `test` split were reserved for the human-vs-model webapp. These IDs live in `configs/webapp_holdout_ids.json` (regeneratable via `scripts/generate_webapp_holdout_ids.py`) and the training test evaluation excludes them to keep accuracy numbers measured on a disjoint set.

## Deployed vs. Committed Checkpoint

The live site at [hanguk.dev/geoguess](https://hanguk.dev/geoguess/) currently serves predictions generated by the **older** checkpoint (`data/best_model.pt`, 32.1% on the 1,000-image benchmark), not the committed EfficientNet-B0 checkpoint (`data/best_model_efficientnet_b0.pt`, 64.7%). The 2,579 human guesses collected during the study were scored against that older model.

Both checkpoints are kept in the repo so the divergence is auditable: the analysis in the notebook deliberately uses the newer checkpoint because its predictions are what the McNemar's-test comparison is actually about, while the older-model numbers are preserved in the results table for context. See `DEPLOYMENT.md` for how to push the newer checkpoint live.

## Limitations & Known Biases

- **Training-set country imbalance.** Even after class filtering, the OSV-5M train split over-represents the United States, Russia, and Western Europe. Per-country test accuracy is not uniform — see the confusion matrix in `outputs/confusion_matrix.png` for the dominant regional confusions (typically between neighboring countries).
- **Visual shortcuts, not geography.** The model learns vegetation, road signs, architecture, and driving side. It does not read GPS metadata, camera direction, or EXIF. Images stripped of street-level context (e.g., plain sky, interior shots) degrade accuracy heavily.
- **Class coverage in the benchmark.** All 180 countries are trainable and predictable by the committed model, but only 120 of them appear as ground-truth labels in the 1,000-image webapp benchmark set. The webapp's guess dropdown offers 161 countries (a legacy constraint from the older deployed model); 19 of the 180 trained countries are never selectable as a human's answer. This is a webapp-UI artifact, not a model-capability limitation.
- **Single-image inference.** Unlike a human GeoGuessr player who can pan and explore, the model sees one fixed 224×224 crop.
- **Small human cohort.** The 2,579 paired guesses came from 5 participants. Individual N ranges from ~50 to ~144, which limits per-participant statistical power even though aggregate effects are strong.

## Intended Use

- Reproducing the CDS 403 case-study comparison between a fine-tuned CNN and human players on a held-out OSV-5M slice.
- Portfolio demonstration of transfer learning for a 180-class visual task at modest compute.
- Educational use for students exploring McNemar's test on paired classification outcomes.

## Out of Scope

- Production geolocation. The model has a hard ceiling on accuracy and known regional biases.
- Privacy-sensitive inference (e.g., de-anonymizing a location photo) — the training set is street-view imagery with no people-identification signal, but the coarse country output is still not suitable for any high-stakes use.
